# Grundstück Hausplaner

Interactive tool for finding a good spot for a house on a Bavarian parcel (Flurstück).

**Open it:** https://bauerste.github.io/hausplaner/

- Shows the official ALKIS® Parzellarkarte, aerial photo (DOP20) and Webkarte Bayern.
- Select a parcel by clicking inside it, or trace its corners.
- Try house length, width and orientation; see distances to every boundary, footprint and GRZ.
- Rough setback check (BayBO Art. 6 rule of thumb: 0.4 · H, at least 3 m). Planning aid only.
- Works with mouse and touch (iPad). Plans are saved in the browser on each device.
